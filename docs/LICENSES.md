# Asset Licenses and Credits

This document records the licensing and attribution information for
third-party textures used in the 3D solar system project.

## 1. Solar System Scope Textures {#1-solar-system-scope-textures}

-   **Source:** Solar System Scope
-   **Website:** <https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/>
-   **License stated for the texture collection:** Creative Commons
    Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
-   **Attribution required:** Yes.
-   **Potential assets used:** Planet and Sun surface textures,
    Saturn\'s ring texture, and Milky Way imagery, where downloaded from
    this source.

### Attribution requirements

When using or modifying CC BY 4.0 material:

-   Credit the creator in the form requested by the source.
-   Include the source or asset-page link.
-   Include a link to the CC BY 4.0 license.
-   Indicate whether the asset was modified (for example, resized or
    converted to WebP).
-   Do not imply that the creator endorses this project.

License text: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

**Important:** Before release, copy the exact creator/attribution
wording from the relevant Solar System Scope download page. A website
name alone may not satisfy the attribution requirements.

## 5. Project Modifications {#5-project-modifications}

including:

-   Resizing to 1K, 2K, or 4K versions.
-   Converting image formats, such as JPEG or TIFF to WebP.

-   Adjusting or preparing maps for the Three.js rendering pipeline.
