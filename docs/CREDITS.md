## 2. Scientific Data & Ephemerides

1. **Planetary Ephemeris:** NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) Analytical Keplerian Elements by E. M. Standish (1992).
2. **Exoplanet Systems:** NASA Exoplanet Archive (Caltech / IPAC), verified radial velocity and transit parameters.
3. **Space Weather Records:** NASA Community Coordinated Modeling Center (CCMC) / Space Weather Database of Notifications, Knowledge, Information (DONKI).
4. **Physical & Gravitational Constants:** Committee on Data for Science and Technology (CODATA 2018) & International Astronomical Union (IAU 2015).
5. **Earth Gravity Model:** National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA) / NASA EGM96 and WGS-84 reference ellipsoid.
